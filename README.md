# CMPM-120-Project-4

Slimes, Wolves, and MAGIC

Alina Stasi & Jerry Zhao

We have made a tower defense game. The gameplay mechanics are very simple. Using the mouse, you can click on the build button and you will get four tower options to choose from. The tower's cost and whether or not you can afford it (grayscale button vs colored button being displayed) is also shown. When you can afford a tower, click on the tower button that you want to build and it will be built. The towers can also be clicked to display their range (the green circle), an upgrade button option (with the cost and whether you can afford it being displayed), and a delete tower button. The upgrade button boosts certain stats depending on the tower: the basic shooter will shoot faster, the debuff shooter will slow the enemies down even more than normal, the range shooter will have increased damage, and the moving shooter will shoot more bullets at once than before. Of course, the delete button will delete the tower, giving back the build button and also refunding some currency. The player is given 1 currency every second as well as gain some currency when enemies are defeated.

We have implemented all of the following UI: towers available to place down with cost shown (when you click on the build button), currency, health of player, health of enemy (over the enemy's head), and wave number.

Enemy & Tower Animations : 1 point
All the towers have idle animations, and all the enemies have moving animations.

2 Basic Enemies Implemented : 1.5 points
We have the slime enemy implemented and the wolf enemy implemented. The slime has more health than the wolf and the wolf is faster than the slime.

4 Towers Implemented : 2 points
We have all 4 towers implemented. The basic shooter (it just shoots at the enemy), the debuff shooter (shoots at enemy and slows them down), the range shooter (has a bigger range than the other towers), and the moving shooter (moves in a circle and fires out bullets in all directions).

Upgrades for Towers : 1.5
When you click on the tower, you can see they have an upgrade avaiable for that specific tower: the basic shooter will shoot faster, the debuff shooter will slow the enemies down even more than normal, the range shooter will have increased damage, and the moving shooter will shoot more bullets at once than before.

Special Enemy (Alina) : 3
My boss enemy functions just like the normal enemies (they have a health bar, walk along the path, have an animation) and my boss has a special debuffing power that is applied to the towers. When the towers are in my boss's range, their attack speed is slowed down by 70% (ex. attack speed of 500 goes to 850, it really isn't as slow as it sounds because this is in milliseconds) and the towers are tinted blue to help indicate to the player that their towers are being debuffed.
